Are kids allowed?

Yes, kids are allowed! There will be plenty of fun activities and quiet places available if needed.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation will list everyone included, including any plus-ones. If a plus-one isn't listed, we kindly ask that you join us on your own. We appreciate your understanding and can't wait to celebrate with you!

Will there be alcohol?

Alcohol will not be served at the wedding.

Will there be food?

Yes! We'll have a grazing table available for all our guests to enjoy, and it will be nut-free. If you have any other allergies or dietary restrictions, please let us know ahead of time. We'll do our best to accommodate your needs!

What should I wear?

Sunday Best to Whimsical Maximalist

Do you have a fancy prom dress, cape, or feather boa that you love but never get the chance to wear? Now's your time! We encourage you to dress up, go all out, and embrace your most extravagant, whimsical self. Think Fancy Nancy, over-the-top accessories, dramatic outfits, and a little extra sparkle!

Need some inspiration? You could don a fancy hat, bow ties, sequins, tulle, pearls, ruffles, velvet, funky patterns, statement jewelry, or anything delightfully extra.

Whether you show up in a floor-length gown, a fabulous suit, your favorite Sunday-best outfit, or just a nice tie, there is no wrong way to dress. There may even be some dancing, so keep that in mind when choosing your outfit and shoes! Go as fancy or as whimsical as you'd like, but most importantly, wear whatever makes you feel your most fabulous and comfortable!
